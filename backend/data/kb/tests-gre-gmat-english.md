## GRE General Test
Used for MS and some MBA programs. Verbal and Quant are each scored 130–170 (total 260–340), plus Analytical Writing 0–6. The test takes about 2 hours. Scores are valid for 5 years. Many US universities made the GRE optional after 2020, but competitive CS, data science and engineering programs still value a strong Quant score (165+). Plan 2–3 months of preparation and leave time for a retake.

## GMAT Focus Edition
Used for MBA and business master's. Scored 205–805 with Quant, Verbal and Data Insights sections. Valid for 5 years. Top MBA programs typically see averages in the mid-600s to low-700s on the Focus scale. ISB and many Indian executive programs also accept GMAT.

## IELTS
Bands from 0 to 9 for Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking. Most master's programs ask for 6.5 overall with no band below 6.0; top UK and Australian programs may ask for 7.0. Scores are valid for 2 years. For UK visa purposes some courses need "IELTS for UKVI".

## TOEFL iBT
Widely accepted in the US and Canada. ETS moved TOEFL iBT to a 1–6 band scale in 2026 and reports a 0–120 equivalent during the transition; many universities still state requirements on the 0–120 scale (often 90–100+ for master's). Scores are valid for 2 years.

## PTE Academic and Duolingo
PTE Academic (scored 10–90) is accepted by most UK, Australian and many Canadian and US universities and gives results within a few days. The Duolingo English Test (scored 10–160) is taken online and is accepted by many US universities, but check each program and any visa rules before relying on it.

## Which tests do I need?
MS in the US: GRE (if required or recommended) + TOEFL/IELTS. UK, Ireland, Australia master's: usually no GRE, only English. Germany: English test for English-taught programs; some TU programs ask for GRE. MBA abroad: GMAT or GRE + English. India: CAT/XAT/GMAT for management, GATE for M.Tech.
