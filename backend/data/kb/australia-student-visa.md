## How the subclass 500 visa works
After accepting an offer you receive a Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) and apply online through ImmiAccount. You need Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) for the whole stay, and you must meet the Genuine Student requirement (which replaced the older GTE statement in 2024) by answering questions about your study plans, ties and finances. The visa fee rose sharply between 2024 and 2026; check the current fee on the Home Affairs website before applying.

## Financial capacity
You must show 12 months of living costs (AUD 29,710 for the main applicant), plus first-year tuition and travel. Evidence can include savings, an education loan from a financial institution, or a parent's income meeting the threshold.

## English
The visa needs an accepted English test (for example IELTS around 6.0 overall, or equivalent); universities often ask for 6.5 for postgraduate study.

## Working while studying
Students can work up to 48 hours per fortnight during study periods and unlimited hours during scheduled course breaks.

## Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485)
Master's by coursework graduates can usually get 2 years of post-study work (longer for research degrees and some regional study locations). Age limits and English requirements apply, so check eligibility before planning on it.
