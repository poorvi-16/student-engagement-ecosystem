## What PathPilot does
PathPilot guides Indian students from choosing where to study to funding it. The onboarding quiz builds your profile and Profile Strength score. The Career Navigator suggests ambitious, target and safe programs with estimated admit chances, total cost in rupees, expected salary and payback time. The AI Mentor answers questions using PathPilot's guides and your own profile. More tools (ROI calculator, application timeline, SOP review, visa interview practice and a loan planner) are being added.

## How estimates are calculated
Admit chances come from a model that compares your profile with each program's typical admitted student; they are estimates, not guarantees. Costs and salaries are indicative figures converted to rupees at fixed exchange rates, so check the university website for exact fees.

## Loans on PathPilot
Loan offers, eligibility and lenders shown in PathPilot are simulated for this prototype. They illustrate how a real education-loan journey works (eligibility, offers, EMI planning and documents) but are not real credit decisions. A real lender would make the final decision after a human credit review.

## Privacy
PathPilot uses your profile only to personalise recommendations. The AI receives your academic and goal details but not your name or email.
