## Documents
Passport valid well beyond your course, the visa, admission and funding letters, I-20 or CoE or equivalent, academic originals and attested copies, vaccination and medical records where required, accommodation proof, insurance papers and several passport photos. Keep digital copies in cloud storage and carry the originals in your hand baggage.

## Money
Carry a small amount of local currency and a forex card, and keep your Indian bank's international access on. Know your first-month budget, deposit and tuition due dates. Inform your bank about your travel so cards are not blocked.

## Health and insurance
Complete required vaccinations early, carry prescriptions for regular medicines with generic names, and activate health insurance. Some countries make health cover mandatory and charge it with the visa.

## Packing smart
Pack for the season and airline limits: clothes, one formal outfit, adapters, a small kitchen starter set and important medicines. Check the baggage rules and the list of food and items you cannot bring into the country.

## Arrival plan
Book your first nights, arrange airport pickup or public transport directions, save your university's international office contact, and register for orientation. Buy a local SIM and set up the university email, bank and transport pass in the first week.

## Stay connected
Share your itinerary with family, join the university's new-student groups and find Indian student communities in your city. They are a great source of tips on housing and part-time work.
