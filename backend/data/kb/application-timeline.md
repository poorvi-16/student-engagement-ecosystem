title: Application timeline for a Fall intake abroad

## 15–18 months before the intake
Decide country, field and budget. Shortlist 8–12 programs across ambitious, target and safe options. Book GRE/GMAT and the English test, leaving room for one retake. Start talking to recommenders.

## 10–12 months before (July–October of the previous year)
Take the tests. Draft your SOP and resume. Request transcripts and LORs. Check each program's deadline: many top US programs close between December and January; some have early rounds in October–November.

## 8–10 months before (October–January)
Submit applications. US: most Fall deadlines fall in December–February. UK: most programs accept applications on a rolling basis from about October to March or later, but popular courses fill early. Canada: many deadlines fall in December–February. Germany: about 15 July for the winter semester (via uni-assist, with the APS certificate). Australia and Ireland: rolling, with major intakes in February/July and September respectively.

## 4–7 months before (February–May)
Admits arrive. Compare offers on total cost, ROI and scholarships. Apply for an education loan as soon as you have an admit; a sanction letter is often needed for the I-20, CAS or visa proof of funds. Pay deposits to confirm your seat.

## 2–4 months before (May–July)
Get the I-20/CAS/CoE, pay visa fees, book visa appointments early (US slots can be scarce in peak season), arrange accommodation, health insurance and forex, and complete the loan disbursement paperwork so the first tuition payment is on time.

## Final month
Book flights, carry original documents and the admission and funding papers in your hand luggage, and set up an international debit card or forex card.
