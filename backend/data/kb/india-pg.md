## MBA in India (CAT, XAT, GMAT)
CAT is held in late November and is the main entry route to the IIMs and many top B-schools; results come out in December–January, followed by shortlists, written ability tests and interviews from January to April. Admissions weigh CAT percentile, academics from 10th onwards, work experience, and diversity factors. XAT (January) is used by XLRI and others. ISB's one-year PGP needs GMAT/GRE and around two or more years of work experience. Top IIM fees are roughly ₹20–28 lakh for the two-year program.

## M.Tech and M.E. (GATE)
GATE is held in February. IIT M.Tech admissions use the COAP portal and NIT admissions use CCMT. GATE-qualified M.Tech students at government institutes usually receive a monthly stipend, and a good GATE score also opens PSU jobs.

## M.Sc and other master's
IIT JAM is the entry exam for M.Sc programs at the IITs and IISc, and CUET-PG is used for many central universities.

## India vs abroad: cost and ROI
A top Indian PG program usually costs much less than a master's abroad, and placement salaries at the IIMs and IITs can be strong. Studying abroad offers international work rights and global salaries, but with higher costs and visa uncertainty. Compare the total cost, the realistic first salary, the time to repay any loan, and where you want to live long-term.

## Loans for studying in India
Banks and NBFCs fund Indian PG programs too. PM-Vidyalaxmi offers collateral-free, guarantor-free loans for students admitted to top-ranked Indian institutions, and many IIMs have tie-ups with lenders for quick, unsecured loans.
