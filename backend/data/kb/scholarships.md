## Fully funded government scholarships
Fulbright-Nehru Master's Fellowships (USA, via USIEF) for Indians with work experience in fields such as public policy, environment and arts; applications usually open in the spring for study a year later. Chevening (UK) funds one-year master's for people with at least two years of work experience; applications typically run from August to early November. Commonwealth Master's Scholarships (UK) target development-related fields. DAAD scholarships (Germany) support master's and PhD study, and Erasmus Mundus joint master's programs (Europe) offer full scholarships across several universities.

## Partial and merit scholarships
The GREAT Scholarships (UK) give about £10,000 towards tuition at participating universities. The Government of Ireland International Education Scholarship covers a stipend and fees for a small number of students. Many universities give automatic merit awards or assistantships (especially US teaching or research assistantships for MS and PhD students), so check each department's funding page.

## Indian foundations and loan scholarships
Inlaks Shivdasani Scholarships fund study at top universities in the US, UK and Europe. The JN Tata Endowment and the KC Mahindra Trust give loan scholarships to Indian students going abroad, and the Narotam Sekhsaria Foundation offers interest-free loan scholarships. These are competitive and usually have deadlines in the first half of the year for the following intake.

## How to apply well
Start 12+ months early, track deadlines in a spreadsheet, tailor each essay to the scholarship's mission, and apply to several. Scholarships reduce the loan amount you need, and some lenders reduce the required collateral or margin money if part of the cost is covered.
