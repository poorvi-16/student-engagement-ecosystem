## Student documents
PAN card, Aadhaar (masked copy is enough for most steps), passport, passport-size photos, 10th and 12th marksheets, degree certificate and transcripts, test scorecards (GRE/GMAT/IELTS/TOEFL), the admission or offer letter showing the cost of attendance, and a fee schedule from the university. Work-experience letters and salary slips if you have worked.

## Co-applicant documents
PAN, Aadhaar, address proof, a passport-size photo, and income proof: salaried co-applicants give the last 3–6 months of salary slips, Form 16 and 6–12 months of bank statements; self-employed co-applicants give 2–3 years of ITRs with computation of income, audited financials and business proof. Existing loan statements help the lender calculate FOIR.

## Collateral documents (secured loans)
Title deed or sale deed, the chain of property documents, approved building plan, latest property tax receipt, an encumbrance certificate, and the society NOC for flats. The lender arranges a legal check and a valuation of the property.

## Getting a sanction letter fast
Apply as soon as you have an admit; you do not need to wait for the I-20/CAS. A provisional sanction letter is often issued within days by NBFCs and is used as proof of funds for the visa. Keep every document as a clear PDF scan, make sure names and dates of birth match across documents, and disclose existing loans upfront to avoid delays at credit review.
